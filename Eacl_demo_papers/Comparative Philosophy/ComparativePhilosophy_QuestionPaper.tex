\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]

  \item In Kantian ethics, what principle determines whether an action is morally permissible?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item The action maximizes overall happiness
    \item The action can be willed as a universal law without contradiction
    \item The action aligns with natural law
    \item The action is commanded by divine authority
  \end{enumerate}

  \item Which concept is central to Daoist philosophy as articulated in the \textit{Daodejing}?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item \textit{Ren} (benevolence or humaneness)
    \item \textit{Wu wei} (effortless action or non-interference)
    \item \textit{Dharma} (duty or righteousness)
    \item \textit{Logos} (rational principle)
  \end{enumerate}

  \item In Aristotelian virtue ethics, what is the definition of virtue?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Adherence to moral rules regardless of consequences
    \item A disposition to act according to the mean between extremes
    \item The pursuit of pleasure and avoidance of pain
    \item Obedience to the will of the gods
  \end{enumerate}

  \item Which Buddhist concept refers to the idea that all phenomena are without permanent, 
  independent existence?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item \textit{Dukkha} (suffering)
    \item \textit{Anatta} (non-self)
    \item \textit{Karma} (action and consequence)
    \item \textit{Nirvana} (liberation)
  \end{enumerate}

  \item Compare and contrast Confucian ethics with Kantian deontology. How do both approaches 
  emphasize duty and moral obligation, and where do they diverge in their foundations and 
  practical applications?

  \item Explain the concept of the \textit{Atman} in Hindu philosophy and how it relates to 
  \textit{Brahman}. How does this contrast with the Buddhist doctrine of \textit{Anatta} (no-self)?

  \item Discuss Plato's Theory of Forms and its implications for epistemology and metaphysics. 
  How does this theory address the problem of universals and the nature of reality?

  \item Explain the existentialist notion of radical freedom as discussed by Jean-Paul Sartre. 
  How does this concept relate to authenticity and bad faith, and what are its implications for 
  moral responsibility?

\end{enumerate}
