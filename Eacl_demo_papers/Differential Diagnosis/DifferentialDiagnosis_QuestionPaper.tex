\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]

  \item A 55-year-old male presents with sudden onset severe chest pain radiating to the back, 
  described as ``tearing.'' Blood pressure is 180/110 mmHg in the right arm and 140/90 mmHg in the 
  left arm. What is the most likely diagnosis?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Acute myocardial infarction
    \item Aortic dissection
    \item Pulmonary embolism
    \item Pericarditis
  \end{enumerate}

  \item A 25-year-old female presents with polyuria, polydipsia, and weight loss. Random blood glucose 
  is 350 mg/dL. Urinalysis shows glucose and ketones. What is the most appropriate initial diagnosis?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Type 2 diabetes mellitus
    \item Diabetic ketoacidosis
    \item Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state
    \item Diabetes insipidus
  \end{enumerate}

  \item A 40-year-old male presents with jaundice, dark urine, and pale stools. Laboratory tests show 
  elevated conjugated bilirubin and alkaline phosphatase. What type of jaundice is most likely?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Hemolytic jaundice (prehepatic)
    \item Hepatocellular jaundice (hepatic)
    \item Obstructive jaundice (posthepatic)
    \item Physiologic jaundice
  \end{enumerate}

  \item A 60-year-old smoker presents with chronic cough, hemoptysis, and weight loss. Chest X-ray 
  shows a centrally located mass with hilar lymphadenopathy. Which type of lung cancer is most likely?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Adenocarcinoma
    \item Squamous cell carcinoma
    \item Small cell carcinoma
    \item Bronchioloalveolar carcinoma
  \end{enumerate}

  \item Describe a systematic approach to developing a differential diagnosis for a patient presenting 
  with acute abdominal pain. Include consideration of anatomical location, character of pain, associated 
  symptoms, and initial diagnostic tests.

  \item A 70-year-old male presents with progressive dyspnea, orthopnea, and bilateral leg edema. 
  Jugular venous distension is noted. Construct a differential diagnosis for heart failure, distinguishing 
  between left-sided, right-sided, and biventricular failure, and describe the key clinical features 
  of each.

  \item Explain the diagnostic approach to a patient presenting with fever of unknown origin (FUO). 
  What are the major categories of causes, and what investigations would you prioritize in the initial 
  workup?

  \item Discuss the differential diagnosis of altered mental status in an elderly patient. What are 
  the key differences between delirium and dementia, and what acute medical conditions should be 
  urgently considered and ruled out?

\end{enumerate}
