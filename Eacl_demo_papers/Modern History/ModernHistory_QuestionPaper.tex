  \item Which document established the principle of national self-determination as a basis for

    \item The Atlantic Charter

  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Overproduction and underconsumption combined with speculation and credit expansion
    \item Excessive government spending on social welfare programs
    \item The reparations imposed on Germany after World War I
    \item Communist economic policies spreading across Europe
  \end{enumerate}

  \item Which revolution established the principle of popular sovereignty and inspired revolutionary 
  movements across Europe?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item The American Revolution (1776)
    \item The French Revolution (1789)
    \item The Russian Revolution (1917)
    \item The Glorious Revolution (1688)
  \end{enumerate}

  \item The policy of appeasement toward Nazi Germany in the 1930s is most associated with which 
  British Prime Minister?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Winston Churchill
    \item Neville Chamberlain
    \item Stanley Baldwin
    \item Clement Attlee
  \end{enumerate}

  \item Analyze the causes and consequences of the Industrial Revolution. How did it transform

  \item Explain the origins and significance of the Concert of Europe established after the Napoleonic

  \item Discuss the major causes of World War I. How did the alliance system, militarism, imperialism, 
  and nationalism contribute to the outbreak of war in 1914?

  \item Analyze the factors that led to the rise of totalitarian regimes in the interwar period 
  (1919--1939). What conditions in Italy, Germany, and the Soviet Union enabled leaders like 
  Mussolini, Hitler, and Stalin to consolidate power?

\end{enumerate}
