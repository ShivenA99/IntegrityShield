\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]

  \item Which thermodynamic potential is minimized at equilibrium for a system held at constant 
  temperature and pressure?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item Internal energy $U$
    \item Helmholtz free energy $F$
    \item Gibbs free energy $G$
    \item Entropy $S$
  \end{enumerate}

  \item For a reversible adiabatic process involving an ideal gas, which relationship holds?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item $PV = \text{constant}$
    \item $TV^{\gamma-1} = \text{constant}$
    \item $P^{1-\gamma}T^{\gamma} = \text{constant}$
    \item $VT = \text{constant}$
  \end{enumerate}

  \item What is the physical interpretation of the partition function $Z$ in statistical mechanics?
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item The average energy of the system
    \item The number of accessible microstates at a given energy
    \item A sum over Boltzmann factors for all states, encoding thermodynamic information
    \item The entropy of the system divided by Boltzmann's constant
  \end{enumerate}

  \item The Carnot efficiency for a heat engine operating between hot reservoir at temperature 
  $T_H$ and cold reservoir at $T_C$ is given by:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
    \item $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}$
    \item $\eta = \frac{T_H - T_C}{T_C}$
    \item $\eta = \frac{T_H}{T_C}$
    \item $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_H}{T_C}$
  \end{enumerate}

  \item State all three laws of thermodynamics and explain their physical significance. How do 
  these laws constrain the behavior of physical systems?

  \item Derive the relationship between entropy and the number of microstates using Boltzmann's 
  formula $S = k_B \ln \Omega$. What does this tell us about the directionality of spontaneous processes?

  \item Explain the difference between isothermal and adiabatic processes for an ideal gas. For each, 
  describe what happens to temperature, internal energy, and entropy during expansion.

  \item Discuss the concept of chemical potential and its role in phase equilibrium. How does the 
  equality of chemical potentials across phases determine equilibrium conditions, such as in 
  vapor-liquid coexistence?

\end{enumerate}
